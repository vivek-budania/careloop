Tech stack & API map

Every integration has
a specific job.

CareLoop combines multimodal AI with eligibility, authentication, and deterministic healthcare logic. Here is what powers each part of the demo.

xAI Grok · Vision

Insurance documents

grok-2-vision-1212 reads uploaded insurance cards and benefit summaries into structured JSON for the patient to review.

xAI Grok · Speech

Visit transcription

grok-voice-transcribe-2.0 turns recorded visit audio into a transcript that feeds the draft visit summary.

xAI Grok · Chat

Structured care drafts

grok-2-1212 produces structured visit drafts, prior-authorization and appeal letters, and pricing hints. Consequential outputs stay reviewable.

Stedi · Sandbox API

Eligibility checks

A test-only 270/271 flow demonstrates real-time coverage verification and maps the response into CareLoop’s patient-friendly coverage view.

Supabase

Identity & profiles

Supabase Auth handles live signup and login, while public.profiles stores the matching patient profile. Service credentials remain server-side.

Groq · Fallback

Resilient text generation

openai/gpt-oss-20b is an optional text-only fallback for generation requests, including letter drafts, when the primary xAI chat request is unavailable.

Sumy + NLTK

Extractive summaries

LexRank creates transcript-grounded summary bullets without an LLM. Specialty suggestions, costs, and denial risk use deterministic Python rules.

FastAPI + Vanilla JS

Product runtime

One Python 3.12 FastAPI service exposes the APIs and serves the framework-free patient interface, deployed as a Vercel serverless app.

Future expansion

From understanding the journey
to coordinating it.

Pharmacy checkout

Partner with CVS and other pharmacies to securely share clinician-approved prescriptions and enable easier ordering, delivery, or pickup.

Insurance claims agent

With patient authorization, communicate with insurers, explain status, gather documents, track actions, and prepare responses for review.

Appointment agent

Find in-network providers, compare availability, schedule or reschedule visits, prepare documents, and coordinate follow-ups.

Automated check-in

Automate check-in across hospital and physician websites or digital intake forms, eliminating repetitive paperwork and clipboard friction.

Insurance tie-ups & discounts

Partner directly with insurers to unlock pre-negotiated visit rates, member-exclusive discounts, and lower immediate out-of-pocket costs.

Multi-factor security

Incorporate biometric and hardware-backed multi-factor authentication (MFA) to strictly safeguard patient health records and coverage profiles.
